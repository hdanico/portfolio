Work About

Grace Danico is a Filipino-American creative director, archivist, and DJ who has shaped visual systems for billions at Meta and stewarded the global illustration community as an executive board leader for ICON. Operating at the intersection of brand architecture, editorial craft, and visual history, she builds scalable creative worlds informed by an archivist's eye and a DJ's ear.
