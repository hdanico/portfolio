🚗 Bumperware

I get honked at a lot.

These are real. They’re on my actual car, in Los Angeles, where people have real opinions about a serif. “Honk if you’re tired” has started more freeway conversations than I can count — not all of them friendly.

Tap the stickers · honk back

✿ Take Care

A playlist and a painting for getting through it.

Take Care started the week after the 2016 election — a self-initiated series of playlists, each with a new airbrushed cover, made to hand someone (usually me) something soft to hold. It kept going. The covers were selected for American Illustration.

◎ 10 × the year

Ten album covers nobody asked me to redraw.

Every year I pick ten records I love and redesign the sleeve — a self-set brief with a hard count and a soft rulebook. It’s the fastest way I know to try on ten different ways of drawing in a row.

♪ Melody Lounge

Flyers for a night I also DJ.

Magic Ways, first Fridays, Melody Lounge in Chinatown. I make the flyer, then I show up and play the records. Vertical integration.

▶ Facebook · New Year’s Eve

The one that moves.

A New Year’s Eve moment for Facebook: I drew the whole cast — the wordmark itself, in top hats — and Animade brought it to life. Eleven looping seconds of chrome creatures counting down to 2022.

✎ When someone does ask

The work with an art director attached.

Illustration and hand-lettering for the press — sad obsolete gadgets, overwhelmed houseplants, a whole finance startup that just wants you to stop doing stupid stuff. Same hand, someone else’s headline.

✂ Made anyway

Things that only exist because I printed them.

A riso travel zine about Tokyo street style. A cassette that’s really just an excuse for a cover. The word VOTE, blown up until it’s uncomfortable. Objects with no reason to exist except that I wanted them to.

The point of it

Fun takes intention, and connection takes care. If anything here made you feel something, consider the job done.
