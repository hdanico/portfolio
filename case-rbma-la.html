Case Study · Illustration & Surface Pattern

Red Bull Music Academy Festival Los Angeles

A suite of hand-drawn surface patterns for RBMA Festival LA — built to live inside Public-Library's identity system and hold up from a phone screen to a ten-storey building wrap.

Role
Surface Pattern, Illustration
Timeline
2017
Client
Red Bull Music Academy
Identity by

01 — The Brief

One festival, nineteen shows, no single sound.

RBMA Festival LA ran across most of October 2017 — Ryoji Ikeda scoring a hundred idling cars, SOPHIE's live debut, an Alice Coltrane tribute with Flying Lotus, a night at Hollywood Forever. Public-Library built the identity around “the passage of perception”; it needed a layer of texture that could hold all of that range without flattening it.

My brief: a set of hand-drawn surface patterns that worked at every scale and budget — a billboard on Sunset, a xeroxed handbill, a phone screen in a dark room.

02 — Approach

Many marks, one hand.

I drew the patterns by hand — hatching, mazes, confetti, loose scribble — a family of marks Public-Library could pull from for any event artwork or surface, distinct from each other but loose enough to still read as one set when they sat side by side on a wall.

  • — Hand-drawn, then built as seamless repeats
  • — Tuned to Public-Library's type and color
  • — Tested small (handbill) before large (wrap)
03 — The Patterns

A set of hand-drawn motifs — hatching, mazes, confetti, swirl — built as seamless repeats and color-matched to the identity, so the cheap flyer and the ten-storey wrap are visibly the same family.

A family of marks

Each pattern its own texture — hatch, maze, confetti, scribble — but all unmistakably drawn by the same hand.

Tiling at any size

Drawn as true repeats, so a motif holds its rhythm on a handbill and on the side of a building.

Across OOH and digital

Billboards, wheatpastes, building wraps, social — the patterns are what tie the campaign together.

In use — a different pattern in every event's artwork

Public-Library set each night's poster on one of the patterns — hatch for the punk talk, meander for the studio sessions, whorl for the Alice Coltrane tribute.
